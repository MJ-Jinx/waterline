01 · THE PROBLEM

A tenant hands over years of savings against debts they cannot see

In a 다가구주택 (dagagu jutaek — a multi-household house under one owner) there is one owner and one title register for the whole building. Units are not separately registered.

So a tenant cannot see how many other tenants already rank ahead of them. Those deposits live in 확정일자 records at the 주민센터, not in the property register.

That asymmetry is the primary mechanism of 전세사기 (jeonse fraud).

02 · THE OPENING

The duty to disclose already exists. Both ways of satisfying it overshare.

주택임대차보호법 제3조의7 already obliges a landlord, at signing, to present 확정일자 and deposit information, plus tax-clearance certificates under 국세징수법 제108조 — or to consent to a lookup instead.

Route 1 — hand over documents

Exposes tax arrears, credit standing, every other tenant’s deposit, total portfolio leverage.

Route 2 — consent to a lookup

Same exposure, by a different door. This is why landlords refuse, and why refusing looks reasonable.

Waterline is a third route to the same statutory duty, disclosing only a band. Compliance becomes cheap — which turns refusal into a signal.

03 · THE PRODUCT

One band. Nothing else.

The tenant learns which side of the line the building sits on:

안전 SAFE 주의 CAUTION 위험 DANGER

A ship loaded past its waterline is unsafe. So is a building carrying more lease deposits than its value can cover.

The surface is drawn as a hatched band, never a level — the page genuinely does not know the total, so a precise line would be either a lie or a disclosure.

04 · THE MECHANISM

A chained commitment, not a Merkle tree

A Merkle tree proves a lease is included. It cannot prove the set is complete — and a landlord who omits leases is exactly the attack.

Waterline keeps one commitment per building over {total, count} and a salt. Every new lease must open the current commitment before it can write the next one, so the chain of openings is the proof of exhaustiveness.

This is the proof-of-liabilities problem from exchange reserves, applied to a building. The registry cannot quietly forget a tenant, because the next write would not verify.

05 · THE ATTACK

Understating the total is impossible, not merely detectable

A landlord who claims a smaller total cannot produce a witness that opens the commitment already on chain. The circuit halts on Stale opening.

That failure happens on the landlord’s own machine, before any proof exists. Nothing is submitted and nothing is rejected — there is simply no provable lie.

Verified on preprod: honest ₩5.5억 against a ₩6.0억 appraisal gives 위험 DANGER; the ₩3.0억 lie that would have flipped it to 안전 never builds.

06 · PRIVACY

What crosses the boundary

Disclosed

  • The verdict band
  • The appraised value
  • Both threshold percentages
  • An opaque 32-byte commitment
  • The block it was written in

Never disclosed

  • Any individual deposit
  • The total of senior deposits
  • The number of prior leases
  • The commitment salt
  • Any tenant’s identity

No honeypot. The incumbent approach pools 행정안전부, 국세청, 한국부동산원 and HUG data into one store. Under the 2026 PIPA amendment (in force 11 Sept 2026) that store now carries penalties up to 10% of total sales. PIPA restricts the join; zero-knowledge lets you compute the answer without it.

07 · ARCHITECTURE

No wallet, no fees, no server

The tenant side reads

issueCertificate writes the band to the ledger, so checking a building is a GraphQL read. No prover keys, no WASM, no wallet, no extension. It loads in under a second on a phone at the signing table.

The registry side proves

Witness assembly and proving happen where the books already are. Fees are sponsored, so nothing costs the operator anything.

Why that matters

  • A tenant needs no crypto knowledge whatsoever
  • No seed phrase to lose at a signing table
  • No testnet tokens, no faucet, no gas
  • Hosted free on GitHub Pages, with no key material published
08 · BUSINESS MODEL

A court has already priced this risk

On 4 September 2026 the Seoul Central District Court raised 공인중개사 (licensed broker) liability in two jeonse-fraud damages suits to 60% and 70%.

The 60% case is directly on point: a 다가구주택 where the broker misstated the building’s value and the 선순위 임차보증금 — the exact fact this system proves. ₩108m awarded on a ₩180m deposit.

Who pays

Brokers, per certificate, to discharge their 설명의무 (duty to explain) with evidence rather than assertion.

Why they pay

Court-established, effectively uninsurable exposure to a single factual claim — one that a certificate answers directly.

10 · LIMITS

What this does not claim

  • Trust is reduced, not eliminated. It reduces to “the registry inserts leases correctly” — the same trust already placed in the 주민센터.
  • The registry here is simulated. The ZK layer is real and on chain; the issuer is a demo console. Wiring a government registry is not a hackathon deliverable.
  • Thresholds are parameters, not legal advice. 70% is illustrative, not a statutory ratio.
  • Prior art exists. Blockchain approaches to jeonse fraud have been proposed academically. The contribution here is the chained-commitment construction plus a working wallet-free deployment.
