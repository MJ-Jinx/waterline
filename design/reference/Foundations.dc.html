Why red still means danger. On KRX, red means up and blue means down — the inverse of Western markets. That convention governs price direction, not safety. This is safety signage, where Korean practice follows KOSHA and ISO: red prohibits, yellow warns, green is the safe condition.

THE SIGNATURE VISUAL
Building section = appraised value. Horizontal rule = the limit, drawn as a Plimsoll mark in vermilion. Water = senior-deposit exposure.

The surface is only ever a hatched band. The page does not know the real total, so drawing a precise level would be either a lie or a disclosure.
